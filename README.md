# Showroom Walk

A phone-first checklist for real estate negotiators visiting a developer's sales gallery or show unit.

**Use it:** enable GitHub Pages (Settings → Pages → Deploy from branch → `main` / root) and open the link on your phone. Add it to your home screen.

## How it works

1. **Visits** – create one visit per showroom (e.g. "Eco Grandeur – Type B"). Each visit has its own checklist.
2. **Four sections** as tabs: Show unit · Model & plan · Package · Content kit.
3. **Tap the box** to tick an item. **Tap the title** to open it and fill in specs, chips, notes and photos.
4. **Flag concern** on anything you want to question the sales PIC about. Picking a warning chip (STP, TNB substation, refuse chamber…) flags the item automatically.
5. **Client summary** builds a WhatsApp-ready message from what you filled in. Red flags stay out of it.
6. **Red flags** gives you your internal list of issues to raise.

## Unit types

Each visit can hold several unit types (Type A, B, C…). Price, loan, facing, ceiling heights, view obstructions and layout shots are saved per type. Shared things like lifts, maintenance rate and facilities are saved once per visit. Tap the active type chip to rename, duplicate or delete it.

## Sample reels

The Content kit tab has two sample reels, **Condo** (10 shots) and **Landed house** (11 shots), each 60 s vertical. They play as an animated storyboard showing framing, camera move (pan, push in, tilt, orbit) and how long to hold each shot, with your phone number on screen. Tick each shot as you film it; progress is saved per visit.

## Compare

Pick up to 3 unit types from any visit and see them side by side: net price, psf, instalment, downpayment, income needed, maintenance, facing, stage, VP, lift crowding, red flags and progress. The better figure in each row is highlighted.

## Settings

- My profile (name, phone, agency, REN no.) for the summary footer
- Summary language: BM, English or Rojak, emojis on/off
- Units: sq ft / sq m, mm / feet
- Defaults: loan margin, interest rate, tenure, bank DSR, sinking fund %, lift-crowding level
- Edit checklist: hide items, reorder tabs, add your own items with quick-pick chips
- Display: auto / light / dark, large text
- Data: backup reminder, add sample visits, clear all

## Built-in calculators

- Ceiling: bare height minus show-unit height = how much the plaster ceiling takes.
- Lifts: units per passenger lift (warns above 8).
- Pricing: net nett price, % rebate and RM psf.
- Maintenance: monthly fee from rate + sinking fund × built-up.
- Loan: monthly instalment, downpayment and income needed at your bank's DSR limit.

## Data

Everything is stored in the browser on your device (localStorage), photos are shrunk to ~720px. Use **Visits → Backup** to copy your data out and **Restore** to load it on another phone.

Single file, no build step, no server.
